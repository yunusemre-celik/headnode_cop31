# Heatnode AI — COP31 Türkiye Antalya Promo Video Design System

## Style Prompt
High-impact, cinematic climate-tech & civic-resilience motion graphics in 9:16 vertical format (1080×1920, 30fps). Architectural engineering **blueprint aesthetic** (`#061529` / `#081C38`) fused with the official COP31 Türkiye Antalya palette and Heatnode AI dark-mode app screens. Bold kinetic typography, Natural Earth blueprint cartography, stick-figure & warm-yellow-lightbulb spark motif, glowing spatial network arcs, and floating device frames with high-contrast telemetry callouts.

## Colors
- **Background (Blueprint Navy):** `#061529` (primary canvas), `#081C38` (elevated surface)
- **Foreground (Ice White & Slate):** `#F4F7FB` (primary ink), `#C7D5E8` (secondary ink, WCAG AA compliant on `#061529`)
- **Primary Accent (COP31 Deep Ocean & Cerulean):** `#0B4EA2` (Deep Ocean Blue), `#1482C5` (Mediterranean Cerulean), `#4DB6F7` (high-contrast bright cerulean for blueprint lines/text)
- **Spark Accent (Warm Yellow):** `#FFD147` (lightbulb core & rays)
- **Safe / Solution Accent (COP31 Emerald):** `#3BB273` (Emerald Green), `#5CE09A` (high-contrast emerald for routes)
- **Hazard / Heatwave Accent (COP31 Tulip Red):** `#E52421` (Heat/Tulip Red), `#FF6B66` (high-contrast coral-red for labels)

## Typography
- **Display / Headlines:** `Space Grotesk` (700–900 weight, tight tracking `-0.03em`, 62px–168px)
- **Body / Editorial Statements:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400–700 weight, 28px–40px)
- **Telemetry / Data / Coordinates:** `JetBrains Mono` (500–700 weight, `tabular-nums`, 28px–38px)
